# Job Application Tracker

**Job hunting is stressful enough. Keeping track of your applications shouldn't be.**

Do you hate the job search process? Are you sending out countless applications and struggling to keep track of them all? Tired of forgetting which company you applied to, when you applied, or what stage of the interview process you're in?

Well, look no further!

**Job Application Tracker** is a simple, all-in-one tool designed to help you organize your job search, track your progress, and make the whole process a little less painful.

Because let's be honest, looking for a job is already stressful enough.

## ✨ Features

- **Manage Your Applications:** Add new applications with details such as company name, position, application date, status, and notes.
- **Track Your Progress:** View, edit, and update your applications as you move through the hiring process.
- **Keep Track of Interviews:** Had thirty billion rounds of interviews? No problem! Use the notes section to document each interview, what happened, and anything else you want to remember.
- **Handle Rejections a Little Easier:** Mark applications as rejected and, if you want, receive a small motivational quote to help you keep going. Don't want the quotes? Simply turn them off in the settings.
- **Never Miss an Interview:** Keep upcoming interviews organized with calendar integration so you can keep track of important dates.
- **Track Your Statistics:** See how many applications you've submitted compared to how many interviews you've landed. If tracking those numbers adds more stress than it removes, you can turn this feature off.
- **Export Your Data:** Share your progress or send an individual application to someone by exporting your data to PDF or CSV.
- **Excel Support:** Prefer spreadsheets? Export your applications to an Excel-compatible file for easier viewing and editing.

*Note: Markdown formatting is not currently supported in exports, but it may be added in the future.*

## 🎯 Why This Project?

I'm building this app because **I'm fucking sick of the job search process.**

Applying for jobs is exhausting. Keeping track of applications, remembering interview dates, dealing with rejections, and trying to figure out where you stand shouldn't require a spreadsheet, five different apps, and a photographic memory.

The goal is simple: build a tool that makes job hunting easier, keeps everything organized in one place, and helps take some of the stress out of the process.

No unnecessary complications. Just a tool that does what it needs to do.
